# Phase 8: Team vs Team Leaderboard Zone - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-02-23
**Status:** Ready for planning

<vision>
## How This Should Work

A standings board that tracks an ongoing team competition over time. Three teams — Green, Blue, and Red — each with their own color identity. The display shows each team's total score prominently, with individual member names and their point contributions listed underneath.

Coaches enter points in a Google Sheets tab. The leaderboard reads from Sheets and renders a team competition view in the gym display rotation. It should feel like a league table — accumulated points, clear rankings, everyone can see where their team stands and how they personally contributed.

The leaderboard should be toggleable — coaches can turn it on and off through the admin panel so it only appears in rotation during active competitions.

</vision>

<essential>
## What Must Be Nailed

- **Competition energy** — Clear visual who's winning, the board should make people care about the competition
- **Individual recognition** — Every member sees their name and contribution; it's about feeling part of something
- **Dead-simple for coaches** — Entering points in Sheets must be effortless, or it won't get used

</essential>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- Three teams with fixed colors: Green, Blue, and Red
- Team totals displayed prominently with individual member breakdowns underneath
- Standings/league table feel rather than live scoreboard
- Toggleable on/off from admin panel (Phase 10) — only in rotation during active competitions

</specifics>

<notes>
## Additional Context

The leaderboard is a new rotation zone alongside existing WOD, video, and roster zones. It follows the same Sheets integration pattern established in Phase 6-7. Coaches are the primary data entry users — the Sheets tab needs to be intuitive enough that they use it without training.

</notes>
